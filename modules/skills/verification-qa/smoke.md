## Prototype verification

1. Confirm that the program starts in the intended environment.
2. Exercise the primary scenario and check that its essential result is
   correct.
3. Use a manual smoke check by default. Add automated tests only when a
   concrete risk makes them useful.
4. Record significant limitations and assumptions that remain unverified.

Keep verification focused on validating the hypothesis. Do not imply that
untested edge cases or failure behavior have been established.
